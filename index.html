The ink of Doodleville is fading!

Somewhere past the margins, a giant eraser has started rubbing out the edges of our world. Trees go dotted, rooftops lose their lines, and whole islands drift off the page. The Scribbles noticed first — they always do — and they've decided they won't stand around while their home turns back into blank paper.

Sharpen your pencil, it's a long road.

Redrawing a world takes more than one good afternoon. Every week we open a new stretch of the map, and every Scribble who joins brings their own line to it — a bridge here, a lighthouse there, a very questionable soup shop on the corner.

Pack a snack, bring your favorite pencil, and come as you are. Nobody here draws perfectly. That's sort of the whole point.

Doodleville Collectibles

Each Scribble is hand-inked, one of a kind, and comes with a tiny backstory written by the artist who drew them. Adopt one, name it, and it becomes a resident of Doodleville — with a house on the map to prove it.

Kindness is our favorite color

In a world drawn entirely in black and white, the brightest thing you can add is a good deed. Scribbles share pencils, cheer for wobbly first drafts, and always save the last cookie for whoever looks like they need it most. We think that's worth copying.

How we give back

Sketching a whole universe

Eight founding Scribbles, hundreds of neighbors, and new faces doodled into existence every season. There are picture books in the works, a sticker club, a slightly chaotic animated short, and a board game that ends whenever someone draws a snack.
